Getting to orbit

Full throttle straight up. At about 10 km, start leaning right (east). By 40 km you should be pointing almost sideways. Keep burning sideways until your Apoapsis is above 80 km, then cut the engine. Warp to the apoapsis and burn sideways again until your Periapsis is above 70 km too. You're in orbit!

Getting to Luna

From orbit, open the map (M). Wait until Luna is a bit more than a quarter turn ahead of you, then burn along your direction of travel (the green marker on the dial) until your orbit stretches out to Luna. A purple "Luna encounter" box appears when you'll arrive. Near Luna, burn backwards (red marker) to slow down, then land gently: under the rocket's safe speed, standing upright.

Landing

Point straight up (away from the ground), and use short burns to keep your speed low. Every rocket has a safe touchdown speed: Sparrow 6 m/s, Heron 8 m/s, Mule 12 m/s. Faster or tilted over = crash.
