Launching

Your Kestrel waits on the pad while a 10-second countdown runs. The engines light at T-3 and the clamps let go at zero. In a hurry? Press EnterTap SKIP to launch straight away. The sky turns from blue to black as you climb, and the engine flame spreads out wide once there's no air around it. Engines are a bit weaker down in the thick air and get stronger as the air thins.

Getting to orbit

Full throttle straight up. At about 2 km, start leaning left, gently: a full, heavy rocket turns slowly. Left is the way Luna travels round Terra, which makes the trip to Luna cheaper later (leaning right still gets you to orbit). By 10 km you should be about 40° over, by 30 km about 70°. Watch Air push (max-Q): if the red MAX-Q warning flashes, throttle down a little or the rocket breaks apart. When the booster runs dry, press Spacetap STAGE (you'll see a puff of smoke as it falls away). Above about 45 km, drop the fairing (FFAIRING): it's dead weight up there. Dropping it in thick air can hit the rocket. Keep burning until Apoapsis (shown live, also above the dial) is above 80 km, cut the engine, warp to the apoapsis and burn sideways (green prograde marker) until your Periapsis is above 70 km too. You're in orbit!

Getting to Luna

From orbit, open the map (MMap button). Wait until Luna is a bit more than a quarter turn ahead of you, then burn along your direction of travel (the green marker) until your orbit stretches out to Luna. A purple "Luna encounter" box appears when you'll arrive. Near Luna, burn backwards (red marker) to slow down, then land on the upper stage's legs: under 8 m/s, standing upright. There's no air on Luna, so no parachute there.

Coming home to Terra

From orbit, point backwards (red marker) and burn until your Periapsis is about 25–35 km. Then drop the upper stage so only the capsule is left. Keep the capsule's dark heat shield (its bottom) facing the way you're moving; the capsule tends to swing that way by itself in the air. It glows as it hits the air. Come in too steep or too fast and the Heat reading climbs past 100% and the capsule burns up. Once you've slowed below 250 m/s, open the parachute (PCHUTE); faster than that and it tears. Touch down under 9 m/s on land or 14 m/s in the ocean. Launch, orbit and come home safely to earn Round Trip.
